THE PROJECT

Many cultural houses in Armenia were built as centers of education, art and community life. Today, many of them are underused, abandoned or at risk of disappearing. This project documents all 96 cultural houses, creating a comprehensive digital archive with architectural information, maps, routes, field research, statistics and restoration support.

THE RESEARCHER

I am Anita Tigranyan, an architect and researcher with a deep interest in architectural heritage, cultural landscapes and the stories of places. This project is my independent research and a personal mission to contribute to the preservation and revival of Armenia's cultural heritage.
